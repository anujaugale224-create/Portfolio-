Digital FabLab · Module
Laser Cutting
✂️

Module Overview

A 7-day journey into laser cutting — starting with what laser cutting is and its real-world applications, moving through machine hardware, vector design, materials and settings, hands-on operation, a full project build, and finishing with troubleshooting and machine maintenance.

Day 1
Intro & Applications
What laser cutting is & real-life uses
▼
  • What is laser cutting?
  • How does a laser cutter work?
  • Explore real-life applications (signs, crafts, widgets, prototypes).
✅

Activity: Spot 5 uses in your surroundings.

Day 2
Hardware & Software
Machine parts & toolpath software
▼
  • Know your laser cutter (Y-axis, bed, nozzle, etc.).
  • Software: LaserGRBL / LightBurn.
  • Overview: power, focus, safety.
✅

Activity: Make a toolpath from a basic shape.

Day 3
Design Basics
Vector drawing, shapes & file types
▼
  • Draw in Inkscape/Corel/AI or similar.
  • Shapes, lines, cut vs. engrave explained.
  • File types: SVG, DXF, etc. & import into software.
✅

Activity: Design a name tag or simple keychain in 2D.

Day 4
Materials & Settings
Power, speed, frequency & safety
▼
  • Materials used: acrylic, wood, cardboard, etc.
  • Key settings: Power, Speed, Frequency, Passes.
  • Focus & ventilation: safety tips.
✅

Activity: Cut test samples and compare results.

Day 5
Operating the Laser
Machine setup & running a job safely
▼
  • Setting up the machine and aligning.
  • Running your file: start to finish.
  • Safety first!
✅

Activity: Cut your design on the material.

Day 6
Project Day
Build, assemble & document
▼
  • Make something useful or creative.
  • Assemble (if needed) and add finishing.
  • Document your process (photos, notes, problems).
✅

Activity: Build and showcase your final project.

Day 7
Review & Maintenance
Troubleshooting, cleaning & wrap-up
▼
  • Troubleshooting common issues.
  • Cleaning the machine.
  • Quick tips to improve cutting accuracy & life.
  • What you've learned + what's next.
✅

Activity: Clean your laser and evaluate the process.
