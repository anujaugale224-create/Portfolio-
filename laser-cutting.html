Digital FabLab · Module
Laser Cutting
✂️

Module Overview

A 7-day introduction to laser cutting — a digital fabrication process that uses a focused laser beam, controlled by a computer, to cut or engrave materials with precision. The module covered machine components and safety, design preparation, cutting parameters, hands-on cutting practice, engraving and troubleshooting, and finished with a final documented project.

Day 1
Introduction to Laser Cutting
Working principle, components & safety
▼

Day 1 introduced the fundamentals of laser cutting as a digital fabrication process.

  • Learned the basic concept and working principle of laser cutting.
  • Understood how a focused laser beam is used to cut or engrave different materials.
  • Learned the main components of a laser cutting machine.
  • Understood basic safety precautions while operating the machine.
  • Learned about commonly used materials such as acrylic, plywood, MDF, and cardboard.
💡

Key Learning: Laser cutting is a digital fabrication process used to create accurate designs by controlling a laser beam using a computer.

Day 2
Laser Cutting Machine & Software
Machine parts, cutting vs. engraving, settings
▼

Day 2 focused on the machine itself and how a design moves from software to a physical cut.

  • Learned about the different parts of a laser cutting machine.
  • Understood the laser head, focusing mechanism, work bed, exhaust system, and control panel.
  • Learned how a design is transferred from computer software to the laser cutter.
  • Understood the difference between cutting and engraving.
  • Learned about basic machine settings.
💡

Key Learning: Proper machine setup and software preparation are important for getting accurate results.

Day 3
Design Preparation
Vector design, scaling & sheet layout
▼

Day 3 covered how to properly prepare a digital design before sending it to the laser cutter.

  • Learned how to prepare a design for laser cutting.
  • Worked with vector-based designs and understood the importance of line and shape properties.
  • Learned how to create simple shapes and patterns.
  • Understood how dimensions and scaling affect the final output.
  • Learned how to arrange multiple designs efficiently on a material sheet.
💡

Key Learning: A properly prepared digital design helps reduce material wastage and improves cutting accuracy.

Day 4
Laser Cutting Parameters
Power, speed & frequency settings
▼

Day 4 dug into the parameters that control how the laser interacts with different materials.

  • Learned about important laser cutting parameters.
  • Studied power, speed, and frequency/PPI settings.
  • Understood that different materials require different settings.
  • Learned how excessive power or low speed can burn the material.
  • Understood the importance of testing settings before the final cut.
💡

Key Learning: Selecting the correct parameters is essential for achieving clean and precise cuts.

Day 5
Practical Laser Cutting
Hands-on setup, focus & test cuts
▼

Day 5 put the previous four days into practice with a hands-on laser cutting operation.

  • Performed a practical laser cutting operation.
  • Prepared the design and positioned the material on the machine bed.
  • Set the required cutting parameters.
  • Learned how to focus the laser properly.
  • Performed a test operation before the final cut.
  • Observed the cutting process and checked the quality of the output.
💡

Key Learning: Practical operation helped in understanding how digital designs are converted into physical objects.

Day 6
Engraving & Troubleshooting
Vector vs. raster, common problems & fixes
▼

Day 6 moved into engraving and covered how to diagnose and fix common cutting problems.

  • Learned the basic process of laser engraving.
  • Understood the difference between vector cutting and raster engraving.
  • Experimented with engraving text, shapes, and designs.
  • Learned about common problems such as incomplete cuts, excessive burning, and incorrect alignment.
  • Learned how adjusting speed and power can improve the output.
💡

Key Learning: Troubleshooting and parameter adjustment are important skills for achieving good fabrication results.

Day 7
Final Project & Documentation
Applying the full workflow end to end
▼

Day 7 brought everything together into a final, documented laser-cut project.

  • Applied the knowledge gained during the previous days to create a final laser-cut project.
  • Prepared the digital design and selected a suitable material.
  • Set the machine parameters and performed the cutting/engraving process.
  • Checked the final product for accuracy and quality.
  • Documented the complete process and learned how to present a fabrication project professionally.
💡

Final Learning: Through the 7-day laser cutting module, the complete workflow was learned — from design preparation → machine setup → parameter selection → cutting/engraving → troubleshooting → final product.
